Privacy Policy

Find Subscriptions, the subscription tracker.

Last updated 25 September 2026

Your email never leaves your phone. Find Subscriptions reads your receipts on your iPhone or iPad to find what you pay for. Nothing it reads is sent to us or to anyone else, and we run no server that could receive it.

What the app reads

Email. You connect Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, AOL or another provider with an app password you make in that account's security settings. The app signs in to your provider's mail server directly from your device, opens your inbox read-only, and searches only for receipts, renewal notices, free-trial confirmations and refunds. From those it reads the service name, price, billing period and dates.

The app cannot send, delete, move or change your email, and reading a receipt does not mark it as read.

Statements, screenshots and photos. If you import a bank statement, pick a screenshot or take a photo of a receipt, its text is read on your device to find the charges. The file or photo itself is not kept.

What is stored, and where

  • The subscriptions and charges the app finds, with the subject line and a short excerpt of each receipt so you can see where a price came from. These stay in the app's own storage on your device, and in your device's own backups if you have those on.
  • Each app password, in the iOS Keychain. It is synced by iCloud Keychain if you use it, so a new iPhone doesn't need setting up again. Apple encrypts it end to end; we can't see it.
  • Your notes, reminders and settings, on your device.

What leaves your device

Nothing the app reads. The only connections it makes are straight from your device to your email provider, to search and read receipts. They never pass through us. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Links you open, such as a service's cancel page, open that website.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier and no third-party trackers of any kind.

Deleting your data

Remove an account in the app and its app password and everything found from it are deleted from your device. To stop access at the source, delete the app password in your email account's security settings. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date above.
