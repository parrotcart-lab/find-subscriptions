Privacy Policy

Find Subscriptions, the subscription tracker.

Last updated 24 September 2026

Your email never leaves your phone. Find Subscriptions reads your receipts on your iPhone to find what you pay for. Nothing it reads is sent to us or to anyone else, and we run no server that could receive it.

What the app reads

When you connect a Google account, the app asks for read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It uses that access only to search for receipts, subscription confirmations, renewal notices and free-trial confirmations, and to read the service name, price, billing period and renewal date from them.

The app cannot send, delete, label or change your email.

What is stored, and where

The subscriptions the app finds are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone, and in that device's own backups to iCloud or your computer if you have those turned on. The emails themselves are not kept once they have been read. Your sign-in for each email account is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.

What leaves your device

Nothing the app reads. The only connections it makes are straight from your iPhone to your email provider, to fetch your messages. They never pass through us.

Google user data

Find Subscriptions' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Gmail data is:

  • used only to find and show your subscriptions in the app
  • never transferred to anyone, except where the law requires it
  • never used for advertising
  • never read by a person
  • never used to train AI or machine learning models

Other email accounts

An iCloud, Yahoo, AOL or other account connects directly from your iPhone to that provider, and the same rules apply.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

There are none. No analytics SDK, no advertising identifier and no third-party trackers of any kind.

Deleting your data

Disconnect an account in the app and its sign-in and everything found from it are deleted. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date above.
