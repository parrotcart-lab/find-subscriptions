Find Subscriptions, the subscription tracker.

Email parrotcart@gmail.com. Tell us what you were doing and what happened; we reply within one working day. Please don't forward your receipts to us — we never need to see your email.

A subscription is missing

The app finds subscriptions from the emails they send you. If a service never emailed a receipt, or it went to an account you haven't connected, it won't show. Add it by hand from the list screen.

My App Store subscriptions don't show

Apple emails a receipt only if you ask it to. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → View Account → Subscriptions and turn on Renewal Receipt Email. Renewals after that are found automatically.

Connecting iCloud, Yahoo or AOL

These providers need an app-specific password instead of your normal one. Create it in your account's security settings, then paste it into the app when you connect.

Cancelling a subscription

Tap a subscription and choose Cancel. The app opens that service's own cancel page; the cancellation itself happens there.

Disconnecting an email account

Open the account in the app and choose Disconnect. Its sign-in and everything found from it are deleted from your phone.
