A subscription is missing

The app finds subscriptions from the emails they send you. If a service never emailed a receipt, or it went to an account you haven't connected, it won't show. Add it by hand: Add → By hand. Or import a bank statement from Add: the app reads it on your phone and finds the charges that repeat.

Trying it without connecting email

Download this sample bank statement (a made-up three months of card charges), then in the app choose Add → From a bank statement, or No email? Import a bank statement instead when you start, and pick the file. The app finds the six charges that repeat and leaves the everyday ones out.

My App Store subscriptions don't show

Apple emails a receipt only if you ask it to. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → View Account → Subscriptions and turn on Renewal Receipt Emails. Renewals after that are found automatically.

Connecting your email

Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo and AOL each need an app password instead of your normal one. The app walks you through making it in your account's security settings, then you paste it in. Gmail offers app passwords once 2-Step Verification is on.

If Yahoo or AOL says app passwords can't be generated right now, the account may be too new. Keep signing in to it in the same browser for a few days, then try again.

Cancelling a subscription

Tap a subscription and choose Cancel. The app opens that service's own cancel page; the cancellation itself happens there.

Removing an email account

In Settings, open the account and choose Remove this account. Its app password and everything found from it are deleted from your phone. To stop access at the source too, delete the app password in that account's security settings.
