Find Subscriptions

Find every subscription hiding in your inbox, see what you pay each month, and cancel the ones you forgot about.

How it works

  1. Connect your email account with read-only access.
  2. The app looks for receipts, renewals and free-trial confirmations.
  3. It lists each subscription with its price and next renewal date, and links you to the service's own cancel page.

Private by design

  • Your email is read on your iPhone only. It is never sent to our servers, because we don't run any.
  • Access is read-only. The app cannot send, delete or change your email.
  • We never sell, share or use your data for advertising.
  • No bank login required.
