Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Find Subscriptions ("the app") helps you find the subscriptions you pay for by reading receipts and renewal notices in your email. This policy explains what the app accesses and what happens to it.

Summary

  • Your email is processed only on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • We do not operate servers that receive, store or process your email or your Google user data.
  • We never sell your data, share it, or use it for advertising.

What the app accesses

When you connect a Google account, the app requests read-only access to Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). It uses this access only to:

  • search your mailbox for messages that look like receipts, subscription confirmations, renewal notices and free-trial confirmations; and
  • read those messages to extract the service name, price, billing period and renewal date.

The app cannot send, delete, label or modify your email.

Where your data goes

All processing happens on your iPhone. The subscriptions the app finds are stored in the app's storage on your device. If you have iCloud backup turned on, iOS may include that storage in your device backup, which Apple stores under your own iCloud account. Email content is not stored after it has been read, except for the details listed above.

Your sign-in token for Google is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Find Subscriptions' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, the app:

  • uses Gmail data only to provide the subscription-finding feature you see in the app;
  • does not transfer Gmail data to anyone, except as needed to comply with law;
  • does not use Gmail data for advertising, including personalized or retargeted ads;
  • does not allow humans to read your Gmail data; and
  • does not use Gmail data to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Other email providers

If you connect a non-Google account (for example iCloud, Yahoo or AOL), the app connects directly from your device to that provider. The same on-device rules apply.

Analytics and crash reports

The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us if you have opted in to sharing with app developers in iOS settings; these never contain your email.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect an account inside the app to delete its token and everything found from it.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
